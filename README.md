# HakoMachi 箱街

**Boxes for a town.** That's what the name means — 箱 (*hako*) box, 街 (*machi*) town — and that's what HakoMachi makes. It's a browser-based design tool for Japanese N-scale (1:150) model railway buildings. Dial in the building you want and HakoMachi hands you a ready-to-cut kit.

## Build a Japanese streetscape, one box at a time

Drop a machiya next to a brick station next to a kura storehouse next to a corrugated-metal workshop. HakoMachi covers the building stock that actually shows up on a Japanese layout — from Edo-era merchant houses to post-war factories to glass-fronted modern offices — and lets you mix them freely.

Tweak the dimensions, swap the cladding, change the roof, drag windows around until the façade looks right. When you're happy, hit download. You get SVG cut files, STL parts for the rooftop gear, and a manifest that tells you what to send to which sheet of material.

## What's in the box

- **Buildings with character.** Machiya, kura, traditional house, storefront, workshop, office, silo tower, brick station, stone civic — each preset is a starting point, not a cage.
- **Cladding that looks the part.** Yakisugi planks, namako-kabe diagonal tile, kawara roof tile, ALC panel, ribbed metal, brick, ashlar stone, rubble masonry, cement lap siding, and more. Each etches at proper 1:150 scale.
- **Windows and doors, traditional and modern.** Industrial sashes and office grids alongside shoji, koshi-do, kura-do, mushiko, renji-mado, and yotsume-gōshi.
- **Roofs that fit the era.** Gabled, slanted, flat, or parapet. Adjustable overhangs.
- **A real ground floor.** Make the first floor taller, give it different windows, swap its cladding, or push it forward as a shopfront. The way real buildings actually behave.
- **Rooftop life.** Penthouses, billboards, AC condensers, water tanks, antenna masts, louvered shield walls — the cluttered, lived-in roofscape that says *Japan* at a glance.
- **Drag-and-drop façades.** A visual editor lets you place every window and door exactly where you want it.
- **Built for your laser and your stock.** Assign cladding styles to named materials. The export sorts every part into the right folder so you know exactly what to cut from what.
- **Bilingual.** English and 日本語.
- **Save your work.** Cache configs in the browser, or export them as JSON to share or version.

## From browser to bench

The download is a tidy ZIP: each material in its own folder, every part as an SVG, STLs for the printable bits, and a manifest that walks you through what's in there and how big it is in real-world metres. Tongue widths, kerf compensation, and sheet thicknesses are all yours to set, so the joints fit the first time.
