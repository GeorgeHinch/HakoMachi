Generator notes / assembly rules

Cut lines are red closed outlines. Blue lines are engrave/score lines. Preview updates automatically. On-screen preview strokes are intentionally thickened so tiny parts remain visible; preview cards use equal-height rows so the layout stays stable; downloaded SVG geometry is unchanged. Parts are merged into one cuttable outline wherever members touch, including braces, rails, posts, and tabs. Cross braces are not overlapping centerlines or separate strips. All structural strips are clamped to at least 1mm. Any strip/panel that receives a slot is clamped to at least 2mm. The outer visual-thickness overlays are separate frame-outline pieces that match the lower frame/rail/post layout but omit the cross bracing, include matching tab-clearance slots, and are not solid panels. Dotted/perforated shelf decks are rendered as blue etched dots rather than full cut holes. The top Component type selector chooses between a full industrial shelf rack and standalone cabinet/component modules. Shelf-rack interior levels now use shelf deck styles only; cabinets/fire/utility modules are distinct top-level component types. Standalone cabinet modules generate a core box/front façade plus separate outside cladding layers with an engraved door front. Selecting a standalone cabinet/component type automatically loads a sensible default scaled W×D×H for that specific component so a small fire box does not inherit oversized rack dimensions. Standalone cabinet/component core boxes now include real tab-and-slot joinery on all six faces: top/bottom plates tab into the side panels, and both a back panel and a front core frame tab into the sides and the top/bottom with matching edge slots. The standalone component core now uses inside-fit panel dimensions: front/back frames are inset to fit between side panels, and side/top/bottom core pieces are inset by material thickness before tabs are added. Tabs are automatically shortened for small parts, and receiving edge slots match the tab depth. For very small components, the joinery also switches to compact half-length tabs/slots so the cuts remain physically possible. Cabinet fronts can automatically use one or two doors based on real-world scaled bay width, and can be left closed/engraved or have one/both doors cut open with red through-cuts on the top, bottom, and inside door edge plus a blue hinge engrave line. Closed cabinet modules suppress hidden shelf/top/bottom cabinet plates and matching shelf-tab slots; open-door cabinets keep those parts so the interior reads correctly. Shelves are split into bay sections between vertical support lines, with offset left/right tabs into the supports; side-frame and interior vertical support receiver slots are generated from the exact same tab layout used by the shelf bays. Interior vertical support parts are now generated as separate cards. Top/bottom frames are now doubled layers. Both layers use open perimeter edge slots for the doubled side/back wall plus stiffener assemblies; only one layer in each pair has blue engraving, while the doubler layer is cut-only. Interior supports use matched enclosed internal support slots. Center/side support bodies are shortened so their rear tabs fit within the requested shelf depth instead of making the support longer than D. The back panel now connects to the side frames with open left/right edge receiver slots using the same outside-edge datum as the top/bottom frames, while interior vertical supports use matched enclosed vertical receiver slots. Each generated part is shown in its own labeled preview box with a material sheet badge. The badge indicates which colored sheet/cardstock/chipboard/acrylic to cut that part from; SVG red remains the cut operation and SVG blue remains the darker engrave/score operation. The SVG geometry avoids name labels on generic components; only explicitly labeled safety/fire details retain engraved text.
